## Description
Pastewell keeps a searchable history of everything you copy, one keystroke away.

Copy text, images, and files in any app. Press Command-Shift-V to open a fast, bottom-anchored picker. Search, arrow through the cards, and press Return.

FEATURES
• Text, image, and file history
• Live search across everything you copied
• Source app icon and colour on every card
• Keyboard-first: arrows, Return, Esc
• Copy-only by default: one click puts an item back on the clipboard
• Pause clipboard monitoring at any time
• History stays on your Mac, capped at 100 items
• English and Simplified Chinese

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Pastewell has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. Your clipboard history is stored locally in the app's sandbox container and never leaves your Mac. Content marked transient or concealed by password managers is never recorded.

NO SPECIAL PERMISSIONS
Pastewell works without any special permission. Choosing an item puts it back on the clipboard, and you press Command-V to paste it.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 13 or later. Apple Silicon.
