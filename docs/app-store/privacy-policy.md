# Privacy policy

The published policy is the HTML page at [`../privacy-policy.html`](../privacy-policy.html)
(English + Simplified Chinese). It is the URL submitted to App Store Connect:

```
https://hanqinglee.github.io/ClipboardManager/privacy-policy.html
```

Summary:

- No accounts, analytics, ads, tracking, third-party SDKs, or network access.
- Clipboard history is stored only in the app's sandbox container and is never transmitted.
- History can be cleared or paused from the menu.
- Accessibility is optional, off by default, and used only for one user-initiated ⌘V and for
  outside-click dismissal.
