Pastewell — Support

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Pastewell is a macOS menu-bar clipboard history app developed by Hanqing Li. It keeps a searchable, local history of what you copy so you can put an earlier item back on the clipboard. There is no account and no network access; your history stays on your Mac.

Need help?
Email rallysis-support@tap-yum.com or open an issue at GitHub Issues. We aim to reply within 1–2 business days.

Quick start

  • Click the clipboard icon in the menu bar, or press ⌘⇧V anywhere.
  • Type to search, use the arrow keys to move, and press Return to choose an item.
  • Press Esc or click outside the picker to close it.
  • In copy-only mode (the default), the chosen item goes back to the clipboard and you press ⌘V yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Does Pastewell need special permissions?

No. Pastewell is fully usable without any special permission: choosing an item puts it back on the clipboard, and you paste it yourself. If you are using a build that offers optional auto-paste, macOS asks for the Accessibility permission only after you turn that option on. The app does not read keystrokes, log input, or inspect other apps' content. The Mac App Store version does not include auto-paste and never requests this permission.

Where is my history stored?

Inside the app's sandbox container:

~/Library/Containers/com.hanqing.pastewell/Data/Library/Application Support/Pastewell/

It is capped at 100 items and is never uploaded. Content marked transient or concealed by password managers is not recorded.

How do I clear my history?

Open the Pastewell menu and choose Clear History. To remove everything, quit Pastewell and delete the container folder shown above.

How do I pause recording?

Open the Pastewell menu and choose Pause Clipboard Monitoring. While paused, new copies are not recorded. Choose Resume Clipboard Monitoring to continue.

How do I uninstall Pastewell?

  1. Quit Pastewell from its menu.
  2. Drag Pastewell.app from Applications to the Trash.
  3. Optional: delete ~/Library/Containers/com.hanqing.pastewell to remove the stored history.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email rallysis-support@tap-yum.com with your macOS version and a short description of what happened, or open an issue at GitHub Issues.

快速上手

  • 点击菜单栏的剪贴板图标，或在任意 App 里按 ⌘⇧V。
  • 输入关键词搜索，用方向键移动，按 Return 选中条目。
  • 按 Esc 或点击面板外即可收起。
  • 默认是“仅复制”模式：选中的内容会回到剪贴板，由你自己按 ⌘V 粘贴。

常见问题

Pastewell 需要特殊权限吗？

不需要。Pastewell 在没有任何特殊权限的情况下可以完整使用：选中条目后内容回到剪贴板，由你手动粘贴。 如果你使用的构建提供可选的“自动粘贴”，只有在主动开启该选项后 macOS 才会请求辅助功能权限；App 不读取按键、 不记录输入、也不查看其他 App 的内容。Mac App Store 版本不含自动粘贴，也不会请求该权限。

历史保存在哪里？

保存在 App 的沙盒容器里：

~/Library/Containers/com.hanqing.pastewell/Data/Library/Application Support/Pastewell/

最多保留 100 条，从不上传。密码管理器标记为临时或隐藏的内容不会被记录。

怎么清空历史？

打开 Pastewell 菜单，选择清空历史。如果要彻底删除，退出 Pastewell 后删除上面的容器目录。

怎么暂停记录？

打开 Pastewell 菜单，选择暂停剪贴板监听。暂停期间新复制的内容不会被记录； 选择恢复剪贴板监听即可继续。

怎么卸载 Pastewell？

  1. 从菜单里退出 Pastewell。
  2. 把 Pastewell.app 从“应用程序”拖到废纸篓。
  3. 可选：删除 ~/Library/Containers/com.hanqing.pastewell 以清除历史。

怎么反馈问题或提需求？

发邮件到 rallysis-support@tap-yum.com， 附上 macOS 版本和问题描述；或在 GitHub Issues 提问。
